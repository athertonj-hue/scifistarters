From Classroom Research to Global Practice

   

SciFiStarters.org translates findings from MA Science Education research conducted by Justin Atherton and Dr Heather King at King’s College London, published in the Association for Science Education’s School Science Review (Issue 398): “The use of science fiction clips as lesson starter activities”.

   

Classroom trials showed that short film clips spark high engagement, but only succeed when teachers frame them with targeted questioning to stop students accepting plausible-looking Hollywood pseudo-science. Every entry here is designed to remove teacher prep time while protecting scientific accuracy.
